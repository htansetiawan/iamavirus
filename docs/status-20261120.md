## What plays: v38

See [December 3 — Understand the bot](presentation-20261203.html) for the
full account. In one paragraph:

1. Keep a weighted list of every board consistent with our view, up to
   200,000.
2. Weight each board by an opponent move model fitted on 148,357 arena games,
   plus a table of 359,889 recorded opponent choices.
3. Screen out moves whose king or queen risk over the whole list is more than
   2 points above the safest move.
4. Sample 32 worlds and grow one shared game tree over them, solving it with
   predictive CFR+.
5. Let Stockfish 19 score the frontier, all of a position's moves in one
   pass, on two worker processes in synchronised waves.
6. Play the highest-probability allowed move.

Correction from the source audit of 12 November: the asynchronous worker
mode is off by default in v38. Earlier pages called it on.

## Found after the last release, not shipped

- **Recapture credit in the queen guard.** The guard credits a recapture
  whenever one of our pieces attacks the square. In loss `ewav2dzr`, the
  recapturing pawn would have exposed our own king to a hidden rook.
  - An isolated, target-specific check matched a reference on 22 real and
    constructed positions.
  - Its runtime cost was measured.
  - It was not shipped, because development stopped on 14 November.
- **Why the search preferred Qxd5 in that game.** All 22 worlds were searched,
  the true one among them. The two best moves differed by 0.004 in value. A
  shift of 0.1 percentage points of weight between two worlds would have
  tied them.
- **Data maintenance.**
  - 164 imported games had their dates recovered.
  - 136 of them had been wrongly attributed to us; the importer is fixed.
  - Training exports gained an option for stable splits by trajectory.
  - The archive passed 186,000 games.

## How we decided

- **Before upload.** Every arena version had a written prediction and a
  withdrawal rule before its first game, checked every 20 games
  ([How we measure](evaluation.html)).
- **v38's rule.** Fixed before upload: withdraw on any forfeit, crash or
  illegal move; at 48 games if z ≤ −2; keep at 300 games only if it scores at
  least 0.543. It scored 0.582 over 318.
- **What a 10/16 screen can resolve.** It passes 22.7% of the time at equal
  strength, and still fails 34.3% of the time for an engine 100 Elo stronger.
  It is a screen, not a measurement.
- **Local results did not predict the arena.**
  - v36 passed 104 local games and failed in the arena.
  - One network lost locally and won 16-5-3 in the arena.
  - The same engine scored 0.77 against a duck replica and 0.23 against the
    real duck.
- **How we compare versions.** Arena comparisons between versions use the
  same opponents, and are read head-to-head rather than by rating buckets,
  because November's rating shifts changed which teams count as "strong".
