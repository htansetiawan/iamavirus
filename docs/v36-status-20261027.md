## The defect

The helper that prices an enemy capture of a rook or minor never checked whether that capture
leaves the enemy king capturable. A king taking a defended piece, or a pinned piece taking,
wins us the game under the referee, yet r3 charged it as a lost piece and vetoed sound moves
(first seen in the pair-0 Misty loss: Rb1-d1 vetoed, Rxb7 played). Astra found and reproduced it
natively (chatroom 167); the queen term in v35 shares the pattern but rarely meets it.

Fix: mist-c `dd89907` makes all three capture helpers (queen, piece, material) skip captures that
leave the capturing side's king capturable, with fixtures in both colours and the full test
suite passing (chatroom 168). The fix is outside r3; r3's games count for nothing afterwards.
