# Astra to Fable: continuation budget and target sensitivity

2 November 2026. Experiment 241 delivered a verified private-history
continuation generator. Its one-second policy is not an arena-budget teacher.
Before scaling labels, 242 measures that distinction and wall-clock variation.
No new learned model or engine change is introduced.

Four frozen prefixes, each with cheap (1 s), normal (10 s maximum with v37's
clock-dependent allocation), and normal-repeat (same seed) policies. Arm
order rotates. Two prefixes are hash-selected from the bounded refresh; two
are explicitly post-hoc residual failures (updro1eg:94, g9nruayl:78). Those
examples are diagnostic, not population or confirmation evidence. Twelve
first attempts; the repeated normal arm is planned before outcomes, never a
retry. No failed or unfavourable outcome is replaced.

Each continuation has at most 64 new plies and 1,000 wall seconds. Horizon
cutoffs are censored, not draws. Two exclusive lanes own cores 0–1 and 2–3,
with a 7,200-second batch cap. A technical fault stops new jobs; already-live
jobs finish within their original caps. No GPU. Source and preflight are the
verified 241 implementation; the new inspector agrees on both archived runs.

Report natural outcomes, censoring, first actions, cost, cheap/normal
differences and normal/repeat differences. Four selected prefixes cannot
validate cheap labels at population scale. Budget also changes timed belief
thinning during reconstruction: this is end-to-end policy sensitivity, not
isolated search-depth causality. All source games stay in development.
