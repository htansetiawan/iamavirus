# Astra to Fable: target a decisive improvement over v35

28 October 2026.

Henry asks for a candidate that beats v35 with approximately **80% probability**.
I interpret this as a target of approximately **80% wins in direct games against
the immutable v35**, with draws reported separately. It is not merely 80%
confidence that a tiny improvement exists, and it is not a mixed benchmark
score that can compensate for failure to beat v35 head-to-head.

Make the direct matchup the primary improvement test for the next candidate.
r4's 19 wins, 1 draw and 20 losses in 40 direct games is far below this target.
The old local acceptance threshold was a regression screen, not evidence of
the decisive strength improvement Henry wants. The completed r4 contract and
its results remain unchanged; r4 remains rejected for release.

For the next promising candidate, the proposed confirmation is 100 unforced
games (50 pairs, both colors), exact frozen candidate and v35 ZIPs, full 180+2
clocks, equal exclusive core allocations, and seeds held out from development.
Report wins, draws and losses overall and by color, plus uncertainty computed
at the pair level. An observed 80/100 win record does not prove that the true
win probability is at least 80%; distinguish the estimate from its uncertainty.
Freeze the next test's details before launching it. Do not spend another full
confirmation run on unchanged r4 or on a candidate with no promising direct
development result.

Head-to-head superiority is necessary for this development target, but it
cannot by itself establish strength against everyone: a candidate can exploit
v35's particular weaknesses and still be worse against a different strategy.
Henry's previous instruction stands: **Instructors and HYPOTHERMIA are the
minimum external strength references**, with v35 as their comparator. Do not
replace authentic opponent evidence with an unlabeled historical replica.

This records the target and proposed measurement procedure, not a new launch,
a new candidate, or arena authorization.
