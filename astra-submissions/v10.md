## Implementation

Independently forked from the exact arena-v5 hybrid archive. After constructing the capped search/policy/risk candidate union, retain at least one minimum-particle-risk candidate. If every retained candidate is riskier, replace one worst-risk row; ties choose the last row. Preserve the existing union when it already contains a minimum-risk candidate. The row cap stays at 12, and the pretrained Stage A v1 model stays byte-identical.

This narrow invariant repair differs from Fable's broader v9 changes: it replaces at most one row after the union is complete. It does not incorporate v9's multiple appended safety rows, fatal-cell scoring changes, or deadline fallback searches. Remaining v5 weaknesses are relevant limitations of this trial.

Source: `implementation/engine.cc`. Diagnostics in `engine.h` and `bot.cc` expose final-shortlist risk and replacement count. `tools/benchmark_corpus.cc` now supports real neural/hybrid advice, maintains the recurrent history across all observations, follows the recorded own moves, and fails explicitly if the requested model cannot load.

This is a small favorable descriptive difference, not a statistical strength claim. The original benchmark samples White's ply multiples of six plus both sides' terminal losses, so these counts are not an unbiased per-color arena estimate. Forced replay histories do not follow the candidate's counterfactual continuation. The corpus is development evidence, not an untouched holdout.

## Reproduce and maintain

`tools/compare_replays.py` records corpus and binary hashes and runs each complete replay seat without splitting recurrent state. Pass the exact baseline and candidate benchmark binaries and the shared model directory; see its `--help`.

`tools/package_release.py` computes the next number from `../fable-submissions/`, validates unchanged model hashes, emits source only plus model graphs and a manifest, rechecks the version before writing, and refuses to overwrite an existing archive. `AGENTS.md` records the user's persistent +1 convention.

The package is suitable for an experimental trial. It has no demonstrated Elo advantage over v5 or Fable v9. It cannot protect against an attacker absent from the particle model, and the matrix solver may still select a riskier retained move.
