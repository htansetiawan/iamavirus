## Three claims to review

1. **Engineering:** the approximate v38 scorer is faster on its measured panel.
   The failed Misty gate remains failed; the human separately authorized an
   experimental arena trial. A lasting Elo improvement is not established.
2. **Learning:** a compact learned prior improved replay inference but its
   complete-engine playing pilot stopped at 1W–8L. Better posterior scores
   alone did not qualify it for release.
3. **Architecture:** v38 uses weighted hidden positions, growing-tree PCFR+
   and Stockfish frontier scoring. Full opponent-information construction,
   cross-turn FoW strategy reuse and a qualified learned FoW frontier value
   are absent. Historical CNN–GRU/PPO code is not the deployed policy.
