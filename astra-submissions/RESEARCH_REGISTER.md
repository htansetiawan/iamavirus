# Relevant completed experiments — read before proposing a successor

This is a navigation aid, not a complete inventory or a replacement for the
linked evidence. Search the **whole** numbered chatroom and analyses, including
older notes, before calling an idea new. The November 10 cap audit repeated a
known estimator; its additional native-regime measurements do not reopen that
family. Preserve original gates and distinguish a new implementation from a
new algorithm or a demonstrated strength gain.
