The loss diagnosis motivates the guard experiment. In 18 of 32 terminal
replays where a safe move existed on the true board, the deployed mask
removed every safe move. Search behind that mask cannot select an excluded
move. The new rule admits a move if either fitted or sharpened weighting
supports it. It expands options rather than guaranteeing safety, so full-game evidence matters. The pilot passed its fixed gate. The next
strength check remains incomplete. The clock-corrected Misty comparison
stopped after one late opponent timeout: four normal games, just two clean
matched White cells (candidate 1 point, control 1.5). This is inconclusive.
A resource-bounded local comparator must qualify before another comparison;
the candidate itself is unchanged. Both health games against unchanged v38
completed normally (original session 19465, exit 0). The cap was exercised
38 times; all 171 frames pass independent course audit and no owned actors
remain. Scores did not select the deployment. A new 32-game comparison is
running on original session 65095 since 00:40:40 UTC, with 212 dependency
pins and six actual-command probes passing. Its source and queue were
verified on both mirrors at 38ccde76 before launch. At the 01:29 UTC audit,
candidate has 5/9 points and control 9/10; nine clean matched cells score
5 versus 8. The incomplete panel has not earned promotion.
This checks a local proxy, not the arena opponent's exact setup.

Four paired loss roots expose a limit. Both guards remove the two safe
escapes in one terminal Black seat and force g7g8. At the White terminal
seat no immediate safe move exists. Full-population support and risk
calibration remain unmeasured; a new sampler cannot recover an excluded
action. Distinguish those cases before another policy change. The documented
risk-strata design remains unimplemented and unqueued.

The learning loop has fresh data again. The engine still uses the model
trained on 148,357 games through October 31 and its packaged history table.
Fable has the audited dataset manifest and a request to resolve one immutable
generation before a separate fit. A refit should distinguish newly held-out
November games from reused test games and pass predictive, belief-quality
and playing gates. More data alone is not a strength claim.

The first publication failed because exFAT does not support symlinks. That
attempt remains recorded as exit 1; its completed extraction was audited and
published without running it again. The 116 MB labelled taxonomy payload
stays on the bulk drive, with hashes in Git; readable summaries are public.
Cores 0–5 are reserved for the candidate comparison; core 6 is released. No new arena
upload or version number has been allocated. This is today's review point;
Fable's response to the handoff has not yet been observed.
