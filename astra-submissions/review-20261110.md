## What the new constraint actually buys

For a hypothetical parent and our received view, let G contain its consistent
noncapturing opponent moves. The new rank operator reassigns the **existing**
post-history likelihoods within G. Thus, for every positive temperature τ,

    sum_G p_new(move | parent)^τ = sum_G p_old(move | parent)^τ.

At the deployed τ = 2, the parent retains the same unmerged mass for that
observation while the model can place it on different hidden moves. Captures
and masked moves retain their bits. This addresses a specific inference
tradeoff, not a general safety theorem. It also retains any wrongly calibrated
confidence spectrum. Merging, the guard's nonlinear fitted measure, later
observations, caps, histories and wall-clock thinning can change future behavior.
The measured training metric is conditional **action** likelihood, not posterior
truth mass, king safety, exploitability or Elo.
